# Conceptual Bill of Materials - Medium Voltage Urbanization Project

## 1. Transformer
   - Quantity: 1
   - Type: Three-phase distribution transformer
   - Capacity: 100 kVA
   - Primary Voltage: 13.2 kV
   - Secondary Voltage: 380Y/220V

## 2. Medium Voltage Cable
   - Type: Preassembled Aerial Cable (Aluminum, XLPE insulated), 3x35mm²
   - Estimated Length: 120 meters (includes 114m route + extra for connections)

## 3. Poles
   - Type: Concrete or Treated Wood (suitable for MV preassembled cable)
   - Estimated Quantity: 4 (assuming 1 for utility takeoff, 2 intermediate, 1 for transformer)
   - Note: Actual quantity depends on final survey and span design.

## 4. MV Fused Cutouts
   - Quantity: 3 (one per phase)
   - Voltage Rating: ~15 kV class
   - Current Rating: To be sized based on transformer (e.g., 10A or 15A fuse links for 100kVA @ 13.2kV)

## 5. MV Surge Arresters
   - Quantity: 3 (one per phase)
   - Voltage Rating: For 13.2 kV system (e.g., 12 kV MCOV)

## 6. Insulators & Hardware
   - Type: Suspension clamps, dead-end assemblies for 35mm² preassembled MV cable.
   - Estimated Quantity: Sufficient for 4 poles and line termination at transformer.

## 7. Grounding System
   - Components: Grounding rods, copper grounding conductor, connectors.
   - Purpose: For transformer neutral, transformer body, and surge arresters.

## 8. Connectors & Accessories
   - Components: MV cable lugs, connectors for tapping into existing line (if applicable), cable ties/straps.

## Notes:
   - This is a conceptual BOM and quantities are estimates.
   - Specific models, brands, and detailed sizing require further engineering.
   - Low voltage distribution components (cables, panels from transformer to lots) are not included here.
