# Medium Voltage Electrical Project Design Summary

## 1. Project Overview
   - Objective: Design a medium voltage supply for a new urbanization of 20 lots.
   - Medium Voltage Level: 13.2 kV
   - Total Estimated Power Demand (Simultaneous): 76.1 kVA

## 2. Load Details
   - Number of Lots: 20
   - Active Power per Lot: 7 kW
   - Power Factor: 0.92
   - Apparent Power per Lot: 7.61 kVA
   - Simultaneity Factor Applied: 0.5

## 3. Transformer Configuration
   - Configuration: Single Transformer for Urbanization
   - Capacity: 100 kVA
   - Primary Voltage: 13.2 kV
   - Secondary Voltage: 380Y/220 V
   - Rationale: A single transformer is chosen to meet the total calculated demand of 76.1 kVA, providing some spare capacity.

## 4. Medium Voltage Network (to Transformer)
   - Layout Type: Radial
   - Installation Method: Overhead
   - Conductor Type: Preassembled Aerial Cable (Aluminum, XLPE insulated)
   - Conductor Specification: 3x35mm²
   - Line Length: 114 meters
   - Rationale: Radial layout for simplicity. Overhead as specified. Conductor type and size as specified by user requirements.

## 5. Protection Scheme (MV)
   - Overcurrent Protection: Fused Cutouts (Seccionador Fusible) at the transformer connection.
   - Overvoltage Protection: Surge Arresters at the transformer connection.
   - Key Components: Appropriate insulators and hardware for preassembled MV aerial cable.
   - Rationale: Standard protection for a transformer connected to an overhead MV line.

## 6. Key Assumptions
   - The power demand per lot (7kW @ 0.92 PF) is accurate.
   - The simultaneity factor of 0.5 is appropriate for this type of development.
   - The 114m line length for the MV cable is correct.
   - The utility point of connection can support the additional 76.1 kVA load at 13.2 kV.
   - Local regulations and standards will be adhered to for final design and construction (this document is conceptual).

   - Low voltage distribution network design from the transformer to the lots.
