# Medium Voltage Network Design

- Layout Type: Radial
- Installation Method: Overhead

## Conductor and Protection

- Conductor Type: Preassembled Aerial Cable (Aluminum, XLPE insulated), 3x35mm²
- Operating Voltage: 13.2 kV
- Line Length (approx.): 114 meters
- Overcurrent Protection: Fused Cutouts (Seccionador Fusible) at the transformer connection.
- Overvoltage Protection: Surge Arresters at the transformer connection.
- Line Components: Appropriate insulators and hardware for preassembled MV aerial cable.
